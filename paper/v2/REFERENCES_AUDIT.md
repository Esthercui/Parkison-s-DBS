## Disposition of Version 1 references

- Farhi, Louie, Wang, and Vatsavai retained with verified metadata and narrowed claim scope.
- Ngo/QOMIC was cited in V1 prose but absent from its bibliography; a full verified entry is added.
- “Chatha et al. (2021), Programming … Toronto … Frontiers in Neurology” is not preserved as a valid bibliographic identity. The matching title belongs to Picillo et al. (2016), Brain Stimulation.
- “Foutz et al. (2024), A novel strategy for Bayesian optimization of deep brain stimulation therapy, Stereotactic and Functional Neurosurgery” is not preserved. The actual Foutz item is a commentary, *Forgotten Tides: A Novel Strategy for Bayesian Optimization of Neurostimulation*, Epilepsy Currents 24(4):283–285, DOI [10.1177/15357597241254274](https://doi.org/10.1177/15357597241254274). V2 cites the underlying Fleming et al. primary study instead. The commentary is documented here but is not a manuscript citation.
- The research repository and frozen commit are internal study provenance, not external clinical evidence. Version 1 is the original study record, not an external literature validation.
